Erasmus Mundus Scholarship 2027 | Fully Funded | EMJM Scholarship

  • By Fawad Afridi
  • August 22, 2026

The Erasmus Mundus Scholarship, officially offered through Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EMJM), gives students from around the world the opportunity to pursue an international master’s degree. These prestigious programs bring together universities from different countries. Students can study in multiple locations, experience different cultures, and gain international academic experience.

Erasmus Scholarship programs are unique international degrees designed and delivered by a consortium of at least three higher education institutions from different countries. These programs offer students the chance to study in multiple countries, combining coursework, research, and professional experiences like internships or thesis preparation. This structure ensures a diverse and comprehensive academic journey while promoting mobility and cultural exchange.

The  Erasmus Mundus EMJM Scholarship in Europe recipients receive financial support covering tuition fees, travel expenses, and a monthly stipend for living costs. This funding enables students to focus on their studies without financial burdens. The program encourages mobility, requiring students to study in at least two different countries, thereby promoting intercultural understanding and personal growth.

Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters Scholarships | Erasmus Mundus Scholarship:

Study Level:

  • Master’s Degree

Study Location:

  • Multiple Universities across Europe

Degree:

  • Joint degree, multiple degrees, or a combination

Erasmus Mundus Scholarship Duration:

  • Erasmus Mundus Master’s Programs usually last between 12 and 24 months, depending on the structure of the joint program. This period typically includes academic courses, research projects, and may also require an internship or thesis as part of the curriculum.

What does the Erasmus Mundus Scholarship Covers:

  • Full Tuition fees for the best students
  • Scholarships cover your participation costs and contribute
    • Travel
    • Visa
    • A Living allowance 

Where Can You Study with Erasmus Mundus?

Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters involve universities from different countries. Students complete study periods at at least two universities in two different countries, depending on the mobility structure of their chosen program.

Your exact study destinations depend on the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master you select. Programs may include universities in Europe and other eligible countries worldwide.

Check the individual program in the official Erasmus Mundus Catalogue to see its participating universities, countries, and study route. Official Erasmus Mundus Catalogue

Check Chevening Scholarship in the UK | Fully Funded

Eligibility Criteria of the Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters Scholarships 2027:

  • Students from all over the world can apply.
  • Applicants must have a bachelor’s degree (first degree) or be in their final year of bachelor’s studies.
  • Final-year students must graduate before the master’s program starts.
  • Applicants with a bachelor’s-equivalent level of learning may also qualify if it is officially recognized under the rules of the degree-awarding country.
  • Applicants must meet the academic requirements of their chosen Erasmus Mundus Joint Master.
  • Applicants must meet the language requirements set by the individual program.
  • Some programs may have additional requirements, such as a specific academic background or relevant experience.

Important: Each Erasmus Mundus Joint Master sets its own detailed admission requirements, so applicants should always check the official website of their selected program before applying.

How to apply for Erasmus Mundus Scholarship:

  • Browse the Erasmus Mundus catalogue to select courses matching your academic interests.
  • Prepare necessary documents, including transcripts, language proficiency proof, a motivation letter, and recommendation letters.
  • Submit your application directly to the program through its official website, following the guidelines and deadlines.
  • Application deadlines typically range from October to January for the next academic year.
  • Applications are evaluated based on academic merit, motivation, and program alignment.
  • Notifications for successful applicants are generally sent between March and May.
  • Upon acceptance, complete enrollment procedures, including visa applications and accommodation arrangements.

Planning to apply? Read our complete Erasmus Mundus Scholarship Guide 2027 for eligibility, application tips, CV guidance, motivation letter tips, and other important information.

CV Tips for Erasmus Mundus Applicants

Keep your CV clear, focused, and easy to scan. Start with your education, then highlight the experiences that best show your preparation for the master’s program.

Mention relevant research, university projects, internships, jobs, skills, awards, volunteering, or leadership experience where appropriate. For your main experiences, briefly describe what you worked on and what you contributed instead of listing titles only.

Before submitting, check whether your chosen Erasmus Mundus program has given any specific CV format, length, or content requirements.

Want to create your CV? Check our Europass CV Guide to learn how to build and download a Europass CV step by step.

Practical Tips for Erasmus Mundus Applicants

  • Start with program selection.
  • Understand the program before writing.
  • Give your application a clear direction.
  • Use evidence instead of general claims.
  • Make your motivation letter personal.
  • Keep your CV selective.
  • Choose referees carefully.
  • Do not chase certificates.
  • Check every program separately.
  • Leave time for a final review.

Good Luck!
